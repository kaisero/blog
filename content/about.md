I'm Oliver Mackenzie. I work as a Domain Consultant at Palo Alto Networks, which
means I spend most of my time in front of customers' architectures rather than
their purchase orders — working out what a Zero Trust or SASE design actually
does once traffic hits it.

This site is where the leftovers go. Specifically:

- **Zero Trust that doesn't hold.** Posture signals, device trust, and the gap
  between the policy you wrote and the policy that got evaluated.
- **SASE in practice.** Traffic steering, decryption, and what breaks when the
  data plane moves to somebody else's edge.
- **AI security.** Agents with standing credentials, model endpoints as an
  east-west problem, and the parts of the threat model that are genuinely new
  versus the parts we already had names for.

Everything is written from the bench. If a post makes a claim about how a
product behaves, the setup that produced it is in the post, so you can rebuild
it and check. If I get something wrong, I would rather hear it than not.
